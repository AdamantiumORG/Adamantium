# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and respectful community for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss technical decisions without personal attacks.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, trolling,
and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the
contact method listed in the GitHub organization profile. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as privately as
practical.

This policy applies to repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, community
spaces, and public representation of the project.
